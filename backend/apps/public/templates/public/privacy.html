    Who we are

    HolidayOps is run by {{ owner }}. If you have a question about your data, write to {{ support_email }}.

    What we store

    You can read the public pages and use the playground without an account. We store nothing about you for that, beyond the short-lived server logs described below.

    When you sign up and use the app, we store:

    • Your account: email address, an optional name, your language, and your password as a one-way Argon2 hash. We never see or store the password itself.
    • Sign-in with Google or Microsoft, if you use it: the provider's id for your account and the email it shares. We never see your password there.
    • Two-factor sign-in, if you turn it on: the authenticator secret and one-way hashes of your recovery codes.
    • Your organizations: their names, members and roles, calendars, the days you add, and invitations (including the invited email address).
    • API keys and feed links: only a hash and a short prefix, so we can recognise them. The full key is shown once, when you create it.
    • API usage: for each request made with a key, the endpoint, the response status and the time. This powers your quota and usage chart.
    • Webhooks: the address you give us, its signing secret, and a log of deliveries with the response status.
    • An audit log of changes made inside an organization, visible to its owners.

    For security, our server logs failed logins, rejected API keys and rate limits with the IP address and the page requested. Email addresses in those logs are replaced by a one-way hash.

    Cookies

    We use three cookies, all first-party and none for advertising or analytics:

    • sessionid keeps you logged in.
    • csrftoken protects forms against cross-site requests.
    • theme remembers whether you chose light or dark.

    How we use it

    Only to run HolidayOps: to log you in, answer your requests, enforce plan limits, send the emails you ask for (invitations and the monthly digest, which you can turn off at any time), and keep the service secure. We don't sell data, show ads, or build profiles.

    Who else sees it

    • Our hosting provider runs the servers and database.
    • Our email provider delivers invitations and digests.
    • jsDelivr serves the files for the interactive API docs page, so it sees the IP address of people who open that page.
    • Google and Microsoft, only if you choose to sign in with them.
    • Webhook endpoints you configure receive the events you subscribe them to.

    We share data with anyone else only if the law requires it.

    How long we keep it

    Account and organization data stay until you delete them. Deleting your account removes it at once, together with every organization where you were the only member. API usage records are deleted after 90 days. Revoked keys and feed links stop working at once.

    Security

    Traffic is encrypted with HTTPS. Passwords, API keys and feed links are stored hashed, every organization's data is kept apart from every other's, and webhook requests are signed. If you find a weakness, please report it privately.

    Your rights

    In Account settings you can download a copy of your data as a JSON file, correct your profile, and delete your account with everything in it, at any time. If you can't log in, email {{ support_email }} from the address on your account and we'll do it within 30 days. HolidayOps isn't meant for children under 16.

    Changes

    If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top. If the change is significant, we'll email account holders first.
